2. The two confirmed rules CONFIRMED

  1. Set pieces are your best channel. At this strength gap, about 31% of your expected chances should come from set pieces, compared with 25% for West Ham. Quality matters least on dead balls (confirmed in 4 populations). Rehearse 2–3 corner and wide-free-kick routines, and attack every second ball.

Those counters are worth about 1.5× a normal regain (confirmed in 4 populations). When you win it while they are still in shape, keep it.

3. The opponent: West Ham DESCRIPTIVE

9 league matches this season. 15.3 shots for and 12.2 against per match, an average-tempo side (tempo rank 8/24). They win 6.3 corners a match (rank 4/24). Their attacking set-piece volume is the one opponent trait our tests found to persist, so prepare the set-piece defence for it.

What our research says not to do: build the plan around an opponent "weak spot" (a channel they concede from). Channel-specific concession profiles did not persist or predict in our tests. Plan against their strength level and their attacking habits, not their supposed holes.

4. What the plan deliberately does not include

No formation trick, pressing switch or substitution timing. In our tests, in-game style changes and coaching changes did not measurably move chance rates, and "chase earlier" did not survive. These rules are worth a few points over a season, not a guaranteed result in one match. One match is mostly noise.
