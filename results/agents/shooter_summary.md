# Agent shooter: who should shoot?

**Question.** Near the box against a set defence, does shooting beat recycling for every role? Does finishing skill matter?

**Data.** StatsBomb 360, x≥96, |y−40|≤20, open play. Shots/recycles: men's international (discovery) 2,894/3,120; men's club 2,515/3,464; women's 2,571/2,183. Matched within situation cells; outcome is 20 s net xG.

Estimate (SE). Pooled = club plus women. All confirmed results hold at 60 s (p<0.005).

**Verdict: MIXED.** H1 and H3 CONFIRMED. Forwards gain more than CM/DMs; the forward-versus-defender gap failed discovery. Prior finishing skill (shrunk goals minus xG) made no difference (H4).

**Caveat.** Players choose when to shoot, so this is association, not cause. Defenders' box shots are often set-piece second balls.

**Coaching.** Near the box against a set defence, anyone with a sight of goal should shoot rather than recycle, whatever their position or finishing record.
