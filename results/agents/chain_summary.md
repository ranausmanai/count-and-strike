# Agent chain: the long-ball overload chain

**Question.** After a long ball wins its second ball, does passing into the overload lane still pay? Is the chain worth more than its parts? Does committing bodies cost xG at the back? Is a long goal kick into superiority better than short?

**Data.** StatsBomb 360 long balls (≥35 yds) with frames: 14,239 discovery (4,492 on camera), 9,308 club (3,548), 9,913 women's (1,911). Second ball won, with a framed follow-up pass at x ≥ 40: 4,419, 3,143 and 3,070.

| Test | Disc | Club | Women |
|---|---|---|---|
| H1a overload follow-up, net20 | −.0014 (.0026) | −.0066 (.0054) | +.0121 (.0073) |
| H1b same, net45 | −.0035 (.0043) | −.0089 (.0091) | +.0104 (.0085) |
| H1c chain S×O, net45 | −.012 (.022) | −.025 (.018) | −.021 (.021) |
| H2a opponent xG45 per extra player | −.0012 (.0006) | −.0026 (.0013) | −.0032 (.0017) |
| H2b +1 minus +2 or more | .000 (.009) | −.006 (.010) | +.031 (.023) |
| H2c net45 per extra player | +.0066 (.0018) | +.0011 (.0024) | +.0060 (.0032) |
| H3 long goal kick into superiority vs short | n/a (25) | n/a (27) | n/a (34) |

Only H2c passes BH in discovery (q = .002). Its pooled confirmation is +.0028 (.0019), p = .07.

**Stress battery** (exploratory): AIPW plus placebo. Nothing passes.

**Verdict: NOT CONFIRMED.**

**Descriptives.**
- **Retention:** 74% at a level balance, 89% at +1; no further gain beyond +1.
- **No cost at the back:** extra players near the landing lower opponent xG.
- **Overload is rare:** only about 9% of follow-ups go into an overload lane.
- **Underdogs:** they find superiority less often than favourites (36/34/27% vs 42/44/34%).

**Caveat.** Frames are at the pass, not at landing. Matched samples are small. The goal-kick test is unpowered.

**Coaching.** None confirmed.
