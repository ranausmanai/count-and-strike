# Quick restarts: mechanism test (agent "restarts")

**Question.** Do quick free kicks and throw-ins (<6 s) beat slow ones (>15 s) by catching the defence unset; does speed help beyond that?

**Data.** StatsBomb 360 frames at the restart, x ≥ 30, delay 0–90 s (<1% excluded). Median delay: throw-ins 10–13 s, free kicks 24–32 s. Quick/slow counts: discovery (men's intl) 574/2291; women 477/2060; club 493/1244. Kind×zone×channel cells; match-bootstrap SEs.

| quick − slow | Disc | Women | Club | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| H1a opp. goal-side | −0.84±0.10 | −0.75±0.10 | −0.55±0.13 | CONFIRMED |
| H1b opp. ≤10 yd (pred. <0) | +0.37±0.05 | +0.60±0.04 | +0.38±0.06 | opposite |
| H0 net xG20 | +.0013±.0016 | +.0016±.0027 | +.0013±.0022 | null |
| H2 within goal-side band | +.0010±.0019 | +.0009±.0028 | +.0023±.0026 | null |
| H3a att − own half | −.0023±.0034 | −.0071±.0057 | +.0030±.0042 | null |
| H3b TI − FK (att.) | +.0089±.0040 | +.0209±.0076 | +.0101±.0106 | formally confirmed |
| H4 own half | +.0028±.0021 | +.0063±.0052 | −.0004±.0024 | null |

**Verdict: MIXED.** The mechanism is real: quick restarts face fewer goal-side defenders. But no measurable xG gain overall, within defence bands, or from own-half counters. H3b is not a quick-throw-in gain: that component is about 0. The contrast comes from quick attacking-half free kicks doing worse than slow, set-up ones (−0.006 to −0.021). It disappears within goal-side bands (+0.002±0.007).

**Key caveat.** 360 frames show only visible players. Quick restarts show 0.5–0.7 fewer opponents in view. As a share of visible opponents, the goal-side drop is −2 to −5 pp, and only marginal in club data.

**Coaching.** Quick restarts catch defenders out of position but gain no measurable chance quality; in the final third, set up the free kick.
