# Agent "subs": substitution timing and type (Myers 2012)

**Question.** H1: trailing by 1 at 55', is a first sub at 55–62' better than one at ≥70' (net xG from 62', equalising)? H2: the same when level at 60' (60–67' vs ≥75'). H3: when trailing, is a forward-for-defender/midfielder sub better than like-for-like (own xG over the next 15 min)? Pre-registered.

**Data.** Discovery: StatsBomb 2015/16 (1,551 matches). Confirmation: WSL/NWSL/ISL (577), 360 competitions (477) and Wyscout 2017/18 (1,941; no H3). Adjusted for strength, xG difference before the decision and home. SEs clustered by match.

| Effect (SE) | 2015/16 | Women | 360 | Wyscout | Pooled conf. |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| H1 net xG | −0.10 (0.11) | −0.29 (0.14) | +0.33 (0.21) | −0.06 (0.09) | −0.07 (0.07), p=0.85 |
| H1 P(equalise) | −0.10 (0.09) | −0.13 (0.10) | −0.01 (0.11) | −0.03 (0.08) | −0.05, p=0.85 |
| H2 net xG | +0.05 (0.08) | −0.18 (0.09) | +0.00 (0.11) | +0.06 (0.05) | +0.00 (0.04), p=0.45 |
| H2 P(win)* | +0.01 (0.07) | +0.18 (0.10) | +0.09 (0.10) | +0.06 (0.05) | +0.09 (0.04), p=0.015 |
| H3 own-xG log RR | −0.05 (0.09) | −0.39 (0.18) | +0.17 (0.19) | – | −0.12 (0.13), p=0.82 |

Sample sizes (early/late): H1 126/37, 38/22, 35/13, 170/53; H2 196/58, 66/32, 81/22, 274/116. H3 (attacking/like): 246/1190, 40/413, 66/322. *Secondary outcome.

**Verdict: NOT CONFIRMED** (H1, H2, H3). All three fail in discovery (one-sided p = 0.81, 0.26, 0.70). Landmark and stratified checks are also null. The H2 win signal is secondary, absent in discovery and absent in xG, so it is likely noise.

**Key caveat.** Coaches sub when things go badly, and the "late" group is partly defined by what happens after 62'. Late groups are small (about ±0.2 xG CIs). Nothing is causal, and a null does not prove a zero effect.
